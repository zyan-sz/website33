About Us

At Brosenger, we specialize in precision CNC cutting and custom manufacturing solutions for wood, metal, and more. Founded in 2010, we were the first company in the country to specialize in solar energy, and among the pioneers in CNC wood and metal fabrication.

With years of experience and a deep commitment to craftsmanship, our team transforms your ideas into high-quality, tangible products— designed to your vision, engineered to last.

Our process is built on precision, reliability, and collaboration—delivering results that meet your exact specifications while exceeding expectations. Whatever you need—we design it.
